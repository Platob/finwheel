# FinWheel

A prize wheel for OBS and Twitch: money wheels, mini-games, prize wheels and chat raffles, rendered as a
transparent browser-source overlay and driven from an OBS dock.

Below are the 16 wheels FinWheel ships with: nine money wheels, four games (a slot machine, a claw machine, a
plinko board and mystery gifts) and three prize wheels. Each one is the real overlay running in your browser, with
the same odds as the server. Tap one to play it, switch between the **Glam** and **Casino** looks, and follow
**Slices & odds** to see every slice and its chance.

1. [Setup](setup.md): start the server, add the overlay and the dock to OBS.
2. [Twitch](twitch.md): chat commands, announcements, channel points and bits.
3. [Playing](playing.md): games, the stat row, the ×N next rule, the queue and raffles.
4. [Looks & photos](looks.md): glam or casino, and your photos in the middle of the wheel.
