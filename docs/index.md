# FinWheel

A prize wheel for OBS and Twitch: money games, prize categories and chat raffles, rendered as a transparent
browser-source overlay and driven from an OBS dock. Every wheel below is the real overlay running in your browser:
tap one to spin it, and switch between the **Glam** and **Casino** looks.
